GUIDANCE

Take Better Care of Your Vehicle

Essential maintenance insights for long-term vehicle performance.

Engine Care

Consistent oil changes and fluid checks prevent severe engine friction and overheating.

Tip: Check oil dipstick levels once every month.

Brake Care

Routine pad inspections ensure immediate stopping response and rotor longevity.

Tip: Listen for squeaking noises when applying brakes.

Tire Care

Maintaining proper tire pressure guarantees uniform tread wear and optimized fuel consumption.

Tip: Inspect tread depth before long highway trips.

Battery Care

Keeping terminal points clear of corrosion protects overall electrical starting reliability.

Tip: Ensure hold-down clamps keep the battery secure.

AC Care

Seasonal cabin filter swaps and refrigerant pressure checks preserve optimal cooling performance.

Tip: Run cabin AC periodically during winter months.

Oil & Filter Care

Replacing clogged filters prevents abrasive debris from circulating within vital engine paths.

Tip: Always replace the filter alongside every oil service.

AUTOMOTIVE TIPS

Vehicle Care Knowledge

Check Engine Oil Regularly

Check fluid levels when the engine is cold to maintain safe lubrication.

Monitor Tire Condition

Regularly check air pressure and watch out for uneven tread wear.

Pay Attention to Brakes

Unusual vibrations or soft pedal feedback require early pad checks.

Keep Battery Clean

Prevent corrosion buildup around battery posts to preserve electrical connection.
